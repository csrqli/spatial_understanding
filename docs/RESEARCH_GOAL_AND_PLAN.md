# OmniSpatial research goal and inherited plan

**Recorded 2026-10-26. This is the plan for the current Qwen3.5-9B + frozen VGGT-Ω final-model line, not a claim that SOTA has been reached.** The executable rebuild sequence is in [HANDOFF.md](../HANDOFF.md). The dated source documents are preserved in [prior_plans](prior_plans/).

## Goal and success test

Achieve a state-of-the-art result on the **complete official 1,533-question OmniSpatial test**, covering Dynamic Reasoning, Spatial Interaction, Complex Logic, and Perspective Taking. Keep the Qwen3.5-9B backbone and frozen VGGT-Ω feature source in this line. Report one fixed training seed, **42**. The intermediate target in the V3 plan is **more than 60%**, or at least **920/1,533** correct. The strongest full-test figure verified in the October 26 [results survey](prior_plans/OMNISPATIAL_LATEST_RESULTS_SURVEY_20261026.md) is **69.14%**; strictly exceeding that numerical reference requires at least **1,060/1,533** correct. It is a dated surveyed reference, not a guarantee of the live global record. Check new public results and protocol comparability before making any SOTA claim.

Only a complete evaluation of all 1,533 unique IDs can satisfy either threshold. Show natural/free-generation accuracy and reasoning-conditioned valid-letter readout accuracy **separately**, plus invalid outputs, token-cap hits, and category scores. Missing or invalid answers receive no credit. Since the public test was inspected during development, label subsequent results as follow-up evaluation rather than a fresh blind estimate. Forced-letter scoring, generated answers, agent/tool settings, and subset-only papers must not be presented as one directly comparable leaderboard.

## Research idea carried forward

The [V2 design](prior_plans/QWEN35_OMEGA_V2_EXPERIMENT_OVERVIEW.md) asks whether a pretrained spatial encoder can add geometry and cross-view information to a vision-language model without damaging its native visual path. Qwen's image encoder and VGGT-Ω's aggregator are frozen. A small trainable alignment module makes each native visual token attend to Ω local features and to a separate scene context built from one camera token and 16 registers. Normalized, zero-initialized residual outputs preserve the native path exactly at initialization. The Qwen visual merger and language LoRA are adapted in the joint stage. This is feature alignment for spatial question answering; it does not explicitly reconstruct 3D scenes during QA.

The completed O2 model learned from answer-only targets and a broad SAT/OmniSpatial/MindCube mixture. Its full-test **original choice-scoring** result is **751/1,533 = 48.99%**; pretrained native Qwen scored **721/1,533 = 47.03%** under that same scorer. The comparison does not isolate the effect of Ω, because merger/LoRA adaptation and training data also differ. The [V3 plan](prior_plans/QWEN35_OMEGA_V3_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN_20261026.md) identifies two plausible limits: answer-only targets never teach an explicit reasoning trace, and the large SAT mixture gives little relative weight to OmniSpatial dynamic and abstract-logic tasks. These are hypotheses, not demonstrated causes.

The V3 candidate therefore **continues the same O2 seed-42 checkpoint** with a smaller task-balanced mixture and publicly released 3DThinker rationales matched to existing MindCube questions. It retains the frozen Ω alignment architecture. Its objective weights the exact final-answer token 0.5 and each question's reasoning/format mean cross entropy 0.5. This candidate is a controlled next attempt, not a guarantee that reasoning supervision helps; the prior [survey](prior_plans/OMNISPATIAL_LATEST_RESULTS_SURVEY_20261026.md) records examples where ordinary SFT regressed.

## Decision sequence already planned

1. **Preserve completed O2.** Keep its four-epoch, 24,904-update seed-42 checkpoint, optimizer receipts, split manifests, and original full-test score. Do not restart it or overwrite completed predictions.
2. **Finish the locked reasoning diagnostic first.** Apply the same 8,192-token, temperature-1, top-p-0.95, top-k-20, presence-penalty-1.5 protocol to pretrained Qwen and completed O2 on all 1,533 questions. Score free generation and the ground-truth-independent `</think> Answer:` valid-letter readout separately. Resume saved predictions/RNG after interruptions; never select or rerun answers based on correctness.
3. **Apply the full-coverage gate.** If completed O2 already reaches at least 1,060 correct under the locked readout, record the inference result and avoid unnecessary new training. If it does not, run one seed-42 V3 continuation. A result above 60% but below 1,060 is progress toward the research goal, not the surveyed SOTA threshold.
4. **Preflight and complete V3 as one run.** Use two genuine optimizer steps to validate finite loss, memory and throughput, then resume those same weights, optimizer and RNG streams through the planned two epochs/2,522 updates. Global batch stays 32 on two GPUs; no second seed, independent smoke checkpoint, baseline, or ablation queue.
5. **Evaluate the final candidate completely.** Use two disjoint test shards, merge only after every official ID appears exactly once, and compare V3 with completed O2 under the same locked protocol. Retain the stronger fully verified aligned model if V3 regresses. Publish no partial score as a final score.
6. **If the SOTA target remains unmet, continue research from the evidence.** Previously written ideas are explicit reference-frame/objective reasoning, better validated geometric supervision, and a training mixture weighted toward dynamic, viewpoint and abstract-logic tasks. Assess changes on training-side development data; keep the official test labels out of example selection. These ideas are a future investigation list, not authorized multi-seed or ablation jobs.

The current active Slurm execution under `yu025` is carrying out the diagnostic and conditional continuation. Read its live state from `yu025_recovery/experiment/status.json` and Slurm rather than treating old job IDs or partial scores in the dated plans as current results.
